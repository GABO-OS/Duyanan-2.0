## 1. 🏡 Homepage: Warm Filipino Welcome & Live Dining Status
![Homepage Hero & Atmosphere](C:\Users\maureen\.gemini\antigravity-ide\brain\3d5ec44c-727c-434b-98f2-90722ae07fdd\homepage_preview_1792555237425.jpg)

### What Makes This Shine:
* **"TULOY PO KAYO, SA DUYANAN!"**: Warm greeting set against real cozy hammocks (*duyan*) and dusk fairy lights.

## 3. 🌿 Table Reservation: Visual Ambiance Selector
![Visual Table Reservation & Ambiance Selector](C:\Users\maureen\.gemini\antigravity-ide\brain\3d5ec44c-727c-434b-98f2-90722ae07fdd\reservation_preview_1792555302602.jpg)

### What Makes This Shine:
* **Visual Atmosphere Picker**: Instead of plain text options, customers choose between:
  1. **Al Fresco Garden**: Open-air evening dining under fairy lights.
  2. **Bamboo Duyan Gazebo**: Rustic wooden tables with real relaxing hammocks.
  3. **Indoor Fiesta Hall**: Air-cooled banquet dining for larger gatherings.
* **Streamlined Booking**: Time slot chips (e.g., `11:30 AM`, `12:30 PM`, `7:00 PM`), guest stepper (`- 4 Guests +`), and a 1-click **Book Reservation** button.
